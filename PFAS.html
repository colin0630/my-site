Overview

Research Overview

My master’s research focuses on PFAS in ambient air across multiple Taiwanese cities, with emphasis on gas–particle partitioning, site differences, and potential source characteristics.

Method Snapshot

The workflow includes high-volume air sampling, filter and sorbent collection, extraction optimization, internal standard correction, QA/QC checks, and statistical interpretation using multivariate tools.
